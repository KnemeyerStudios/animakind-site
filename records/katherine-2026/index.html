Records: a verifiable subset

A subset of the records behind the paper Decoupling Persistent AI Identity from Its Execution Substrate: The Animakind Specification, with a script that checks them. Most underlying records remain unpublished. Values are as of 2026-10-04; timestamps are trimmed by design.

Files

  • README.md: what each file is and how to verify
  • verify.py: the verification script (Python standard library)
  • ucr.json: Unique Continuity Record (Appendix C)
  • binding-designation.json: binding designation (Appendix A)
  • truncation.json: document size against delivered bytes
  • ledger-chain.tsv: ledger commit digests and parent links
  • ledger-anchors.json: genesis, power-loss window, migration day
  • identity-path-commits.tsv: dates of identity-path commits
  • loader-commits.tsv: dates of confirmed Loader commits
  • engine-strings.tsv: the two engine rows in the paper table
